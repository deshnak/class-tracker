Canvas Check optional

None of these ever overwrite what you've entered and never add a course on their own. They only cross-check the 5 courses already in this app against Canvas: quietly filling in a due date or point value if yours is blank, and putting anything that actually disagrees (or any Canvas assignment you don't have at all) into the Canvas Check card on your Dashboard for you to approve. Classes on your Canvas that aren't in this app are never touched. Use whichever of the three methods below actually works for your Canvas account.

Method 1 · Calendar Feed (easiest)

Gets due dates only, no points/weights. Works even if your school has disabled access tokens.

  1. In Canvas, go to Calendar (left sidebar).
  2. Click "Calendar Feed" in the bottom-right of the calendar list.
  3. Copy the URL it gives you (starts with https://yourschool.instructure.com/feeds/calendars/...) and paste it below.

Method 2 · Browser Sync (most complete)

Gets due dates, points, AND grading weights, by reading Canvas through your own logged-in browser session — no token needed. Requires installing the free Tampermonkey browser extension once.

  1. Install Tampermonkey for your browser (link above), if you haven't already.
  2. Click this link, then click "Install" in the Tampermonkey tab that opens: canvas-sync.user.js
  3. Open Canvas in a tab (make sure this app is running locally). A pink "↻ Sync to Class Tracker" button appears in the bottom-right corner.
  4. Click it. Come back to this app and refresh — anything worth reviewing shows up on your Dashboard.

Method 3 · Access Token

The most direct method, but some schools disable self-service tokens for students — if step 2 below shows an error about your administrator restricting this, use Method 1 or 2 instead.

  1. Go to Canvas → Account → Settings.
  2. Scroll to "Approved Integrations" and click "+ New Access Token".
  3. Give it any purpose name, leave expiry blank (or set one), click Generate.
  4. Copy the token and paste it below. It's stored only in this app's local data/config.json file on your machine — never sent anywhere except directly to Canvas.
